Out-of-sample forecast evaluation: SARIMA, VAR / VECM, smoothing baselines, and the SARIMA + naive average
Rolling origin, expanding window: each model is refitted at every monthly origin on all data up to it (data start 2002-12); each origin is one fold. Orders come from training; the selected model per series and family (SARIMA, system, smoothing, ML, SARIMAX) from validation (lowest one-month MASE), fixed before test. Each family's test is run once, with all its models.
Systems: VAR(5) on the differences and VECM(5) (rank 1) on the levels, fitted on both series up to each origin and forecasting both; normal intervals from the system's forecast-error covariance (no GARCH).
Smoothing: SMA(k) forecasts the average of the last k levels; single, double (Holt, plain and damped) and triple (Holt-Winters, 12-month season) exponential smoothing, statsmodels' ExponentialSmoothing on the levels, re-estimated at every origin. Point forecasts only.
SARIMA + naive average: the equal-weight average of the series' selected SARIMA and the naive (no-change) forecast, from the same origins. Point forecasts only.
SARIMAX: the series' selected SARIMA (same orders) plus the other series' last 1, 3, 5 monthly changes as regressors (the VAR orders of steps V2 and V6), so DM vs SARIMA measures what the other series adds. Forecast months whose regressors aren't known yet use the other series' own selected-SARIMA forecasts from the same origin. Point forecasts only: the intervals would treat those planned regressors as known.
Lag-feature ML: Ridge and Lasso (standardized features, penalty by time-ordered cross-validation in each window), random forest and histogram gradient boosting (trees chosen on the window's last quarter, in time order), each predicting next month's change of one series from the last 1, 3, 5 changes of both series, refitted at every origin and forecasting recursively. Point forecasts only. Settings and their justifications: autoregressive_experiments/configs/hyperparameters.json.
Point errors are on the levels: MAE, RMSE in trillions; MSE in trillions squared; MAPE in %; MASE = MAE / the training split's in-sample MAE of the one-month naive forecast (scales: fed_total_assets_T 0.0337, monetary_base_T 0.0402).
DM vs naive: Diebold-Mariano test of equal expected loss against the naive forecast, absolute-error loss (matches MAE) and squared-error loss (matches MSE), with the Harvey-Leybourne-Newbold correction and h - 1 autocovariance lags. 'better' / 'worse' when significant at 5%, otherwise the p-value. DM vs SARIMA: the same test, each non-SARIMA model against the series' selected SARIMA: does any other approach beat it?
Intervals: quantiles of each model's forecast distribution on the levels, with a constant shock variance, U5b's GARCH, or U5c's EGARCH / GJR-GARCH (each refitted at every origin; EGARCH's multi-step variances simulated with a fixed seed); normal for constant variance, unit-variance Student-t otherwise; naive uses random-walk quantiles. Pinball = mean quantile loss over the 5 quantiles.
The COVID months (2020) are in validation and are kept, by the user's decision.
