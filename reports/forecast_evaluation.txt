Test of the champions (one per family and series, chosen on validation)
Three-way split: training fits, validation (2016-11 to 2021-06; an origin every 4 months) tunes and chooses, test (2021-07 to 2026-02) measures. This step runs each champion, the mean of the three champions, naive and drift over the test months once; nothing is tuned or chosen after it. Rolling origin, expanding window: at every test month each model is refitted on all months from 2002-12 to it, with its tuned settings, and forecasts up to 12 months ahead.
Errors are on the levels: MAE, RMSE in trillions; MSE in trillions squared; MAPE in %; MASE = MAE / the training split's mean absolute monthly change (scales: fed_total_assets_T 0.0337, monetary_base_T 0.0402), so naive's own MASE is above 1 when the test moves more than training did; MAE/naive compares like with like. ME: mean of actual - forecast (negative: the model forecasts too high); over: share of forecasts above the actual (0.50 when unbiased). DM: Diebold-Mariano test against naive, absolute-error loss, with the Harvey-Leybourne-Newbold correction; 'better' / 'worse' when significant at 5%. 100 such tests are reported (absolute and squared loss); about 5 would come out significant by chance alone, so one borderline p-value is weak evidence. LB p: Ljung-Box p-value of the 1-month errors at 12 lags; below 5%, the errors are autocorrelated and the model leaves structure unused.
Bands: each forecast plus the 2.5 / 10 / 50 / 90 / 97.5% quantiles of the same model's errors at that horizon from every earlier forecast since the end of training whose target was already known (empirical prediction intervals, split-conformal style); coverage targets 0.80 and 0.95.
