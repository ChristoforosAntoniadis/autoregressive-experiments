Out-of-sample forecast evaluation (step U6): validation, then test
Rolling origin, expanding window: each model is refitted at every monthly origin on all data up to it (data start 2002-12); each origin is one fold. Orders come from training, the selected model per series from validation (lowest one-month MASE), fixed before test. Test is run once, with all models.
Point errors are on the levels: MAE, RMSE in trillions; MSE in trillions squared; MAPE in %; MASE = MAE / the training split's in-sample MAE of the one-month naive forecast (scales: fed_total_assets_T 0.0337, monetary_base_T 0.0402).
DM vs naive: Diebold-Mariano test of equal expected loss against the naive forecast, absolute-error loss (matches MAE) and squared-error loss (matches MSE), with the Harvey-Leybourne-Newbold correction and h - 1 autocovariance lags. 'better' / 'worse' when significant at 5%, otherwise the p-value.
Intervals: quantiles of each model's forecast distribution on the levels, with a constant shock variance or the GARCH variant chosen in step U5b (refitted at every origin); normal for constant variance, unit-variance Student-t for GARCH-t; naive uses random-walk quantiles. Pinball = mean quantile loss over the 5 quantiles.
The COVID months (2020) are in validation and are kept, by the user's decision.
