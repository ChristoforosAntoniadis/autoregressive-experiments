Tuning and champion selection on the validation split
23 expanding-window origins, every 40 days from 2016-03-31 to 2019-08-15: each model is fitted on every day from 2005-10-03 to the origin and forecasts the validation days up to 2019-10-01, at most 21 ahead. Score: MAE relative to seasonal naive's at the same horizon, averaged over horizons 1, 5, 10, 21 (0.8: 20% more accurate than seasonal naive; every horizon counts the same). se: the score's standard error from 1000 bootstrap resamples of the origins. Settings searched by Optuna (grid when the space has at most 60 settings, seeded TPE with 60 trials otherwise); joint models (VAR, VECM, ML, networks) tuned once on both series. Champion per family and series: the lowest score; models within 1 standard error of it are tied with it, not beaten. Search spaces and their reasoning: autoregressive_experiments/configs/daily_treasury.json.
