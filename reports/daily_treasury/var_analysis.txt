VAR / VECM interpretation: Granger causality, impulse responses and FEVD
Training split only: 2005-10-03 to 2016-03-31. Systems carried forward by the residual checks: VAR(5) on the differences, VECM(5) on the levels.
Granger: VAR, Wald chi-squared test; VECM, F-test including the error-correction term. Null: the causing series doesn't help predict the caused one. Significance level 5%.
Impulse responses: orthogonalized, one-standard-deviation shocks, responses of the levels in billions (the VAR's cumulative responses). 95% asymptotic bands for the VAR only.
Variance shares: of each series' level forecast-error variance, from its own and the other series' shocks. Ordering: deposits cumulative first (main), and reversed.
