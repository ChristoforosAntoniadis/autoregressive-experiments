Test of the champions (one per family and series, chosen on validation)
Three-way split: training fits, validation (2016-04-01 to 2019-10-01; an origin every 40 days) tunes and chooses, test (2019-10-02 to 2023-04-03) measures. This step runs each champion, the mean of the three champions, naive, drift and seasonal naive over the test days once; nothing is tuned or chosen after it. Rolling origin, expanding window: at every 20th test day (an origin every 20 days) each model is refitted on all days from 2005-10-03 to it, with its tuned settings, and forecasts up to 21 days ahead.
Errors are on the levels: MAE, RMSE in billions; MSE in billions squared; MAPE in %; MASE = MAE / the training split's mean absolute change over 5 days (the seasonal naive's one-step error) (scales: deposits_cumulative_B 26.8303, withdrawals_cumulative_B 23.1045), so seasonal naive's own MASE is above 1 when the test moves more than training did; MAE/seasonal naive compares like with like. ME: mean of actual - forecast (negative: the model forecasts too high); over: share of forecasts above the actual (0.50 when unbiased). DM: Diebold-Mariano test against seasonal naive, absolute-error loss, with the Harvey-Leybourne-Newbold correction; 'better' / 'worse' when significant at 5%. 96 such tests are reported (absolute and squared loss); about 5 would come out significant by chance alone, so one borderline p-value is weak evidence. LB p: Ljung-Box p-value of the 1-day errors at 5 lags; below 5%, the errors are autocorrelated and the model leaves structure unused (not applicable here: the origins are 20 days apart).
Bands: each forecast plus the 2.5 / 10 / 50 / 90 / 97.5% quantiles of the same model's errors at that horizon from every earlier forecast since the end of training whose target was already known (empirical prediction intervals, split-conformal style); coverage targets 0.80 and 0.95.
