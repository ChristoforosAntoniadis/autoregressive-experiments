Recommended = best by AIC, AICc and BIC, plus the AIC runner-up when it is within 2 AIC of the best.
Both tests' null hypothesis: no autocorrelation up to the lag. Significance level: 5%.
Ljung-Box degrees of freedom = lag - (p + q + P + Q); n/a when that is not positive.
Breusch-Godfrey is the auxiliary regression of the residuals on their own lags; for models with MA terms this is an approximation of the exact LM test.
A model passes when every available p-value is at or above the significance level.
