Tuning and champion selection on the validation split
14 expanding-window origins, every 4 months from 2016-10 to 2021-02: each model is fitted on every month from 2002-12 to the origin and forecasts the validation months up to 2021-06, at most 12 ahead. Score: MAE relative to naive's at the same horizon, averaged over horizons 1, 3, 6, 9, 12 (0.8: 20% more accurate than naive; every horizon counts the same). se: the score's standard error from 1000 bootstrap resamples of the origins. Settings searched by Optuna (grid when the space has at most 60 settings, seeded TPE with 60 trials otherwise); joint models (VAR, VECM, ML, networks) tuned once on both series. Champion per family and series: the lowest score; models within 1 standard error of it are tied with it, not beaten. Search spaces and their reasoning: autoregressive_experiments/configs/hyperparameters.json.
