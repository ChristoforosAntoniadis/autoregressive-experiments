GARCH(1,1) on the residuals of the models that passed the residual checks
Training split only: 2003-01 to 2016-10, 166 first differences.
Two-step estimation: the SARIMA mean model as fitted, then each variance model on its residuals (zero mean). BIC picks the variance model; AIC is shown alongside.
Break-adjusted: GARCH-t on the residuals divided by each regime's standard deviation, at the variance break from the volatility checks. Its likelihood is converted back to the residuals, and it counts 2 extra parameters (the regime scale ratio and the break date), so all four AIC / BIC values are comparable.
Standardized residuals = residuals / GARCH's monthly standard deviation. If GARCH captured the clustering, ARCH-LM and Ljung-Box on their squares pass at 5% (lags 1, 6, 12).
p-values by parametric bootstrap: 999 series simulated from the fitted variance model and re-estimated the same way (break search included); p = (1 + simulated statistics >= observed) / (replications + 1). The chi-squared tables don't hold for residuals standardized by a fitted model (Li & Mak 1994).
Half-life: months for a volatility shock to halve, log(0.5) / log(alpha + beta).
